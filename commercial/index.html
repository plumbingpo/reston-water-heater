Kitchen Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

Each hour without hot water costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. In a salon, it cancels appointments. For a hotel, it brings complaints at the front desk, and in an apartment building it invites tenant disputes. Reston Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is pinpointing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. After that, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Reston Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
