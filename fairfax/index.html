How Fairfax Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Most premature tank failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like discolored hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both modest fixes. The same drip ignored for months can wear out the valve and stress the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Hot Water Headaches Fairfax Homeowners Face

Every type of water heater fails in its own way, and we work on all of them in Fairfax. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
