How Herndon Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, rumbling, or moisture under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new heater after a leak.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, neglected for a few years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and burn out the lower element on an electric model, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away the moment something seems off. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Herndon

Whatever type of water heater your Herndon home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
